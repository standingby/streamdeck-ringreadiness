# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other
project space, should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their age, body, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to Stream Deck plugins or to GitHub.
- Assume good intent and ask before concluding.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Respect other people's privacy. Never post someone else's health data, tokens or credentials,
  and redact your own before sharing logs or screenshots.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, personal attacks, and derogatory or discriminatory comments.
- Harassment in any form, public or private, including unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions
that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone who does so.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub: open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/standingby/ringreadiness/security/advisories/new)
and say it is a conduct report, or contact [@standingby](https://github.com/standingby) directly.
Reports are handled confidentially.
